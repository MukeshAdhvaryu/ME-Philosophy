> **Status tracking:** Implementation status is maintained in the corresponding RDMP document, not here. MD documents record decisions and rationale only.

## The Problem

What is the fundamental design problem this decision addresses?

A Major Decision captures a choice that is large enough to affect multiple components,
long-lived enough to outlast the current team's memory, or significant enough that its
reasoning must be preserved. State the problem plainly.

---

## Why Existing Approaches Fall Short

> **OPTIONAL** - include when this decision challenges industry convention, prior art,
> or a pattern the team has used before. Omit for decisions that introduce new ground
> with no predecessor to contrast against.

What does the industry, the language, or common convention do instead? What goes wrong?

Be specific. Vague criticism is not a reason. A concrete failure case that demonstrates
the problem is a reason. If the correct design has existed elsewhere, state it and cite
the evidence.

### Deliberating Contributor(s)

What did other contributors argue? Where did they agree or push back?
